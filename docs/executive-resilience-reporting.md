# Executive Resilience Reporting

## Purpose

Executive resilience reporting converts technical assessment results into a short decision-ready summary for leadership.

## Report Structure

1. executive summary
2. scope and assumptions
3. top strengths
4. top risks
5. maturity by domain
6. roadmap and ownership
7. follow-up decisions

## What Leadership Needs To See

- where resilience is strong
- where the organization is exposed
- what can be fixed quickly
- what needs funding or sponsorship

## Reporting Rules

- keep the narrative plain and direct
- connect findings to business impact
- show the evidence behind important conclusions
- make the next step obvious
