# DR Maturity

## Purpose

DR maturity measures how ready the organization is to recover critical services within agreed recovery objectives.

## Key Signals

- critical services are identified
- RTO and RPO are defined
- recovery tests occur on a schedule
- recovery gaps are tracked and remediated
- failback is included in the plan

## Use

Use this page to judge whether recovery is truly ready or only documented.

## Common Gaps

- undocumented recovery assumptions
- stale backup validation
- incomplete dependency mapping
- limited failover evidence

## Evidence To Collect

- DR plans
- restore and failover test results
- dependency maps
- recovery issue logs
