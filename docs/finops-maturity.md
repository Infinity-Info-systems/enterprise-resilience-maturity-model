# FinOps Maturity

## Purpose

FinOps maturity measures whether cloud spend is visible, governed, forecasted, and improved in a disciplined way.

## Key Signals

- cost ownership is clear
- budgets are reviewed regularly
- forecasts are tracked against actuals
- optimization actions are closed and measured
- savings claims are validated

## Use

Use this page to understand whether FinOps is an operating discipline or just a reporting exercise.

## Common Gaps

- poor tagging discipline
- no cost owner for key services
- weak commitment management
- savings are not validated after changes

## Evidence To Collect

- spend and forecast reports
- tagging coverage
- optimization backlog
- budget review artifacts
