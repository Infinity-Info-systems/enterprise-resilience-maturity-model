# Reliability Maturity

## Purpose

Reliability maturity measures how consistently teams define service ownership, manage incidents, and improve service stability.

## Key Signals

- service ownership is explicit
- SLOs are defined and reviewed
- incident reviews lead to action
- reliability metrics are used in planning

## Common Gaps

- no clear service owner
- weak alert hygiene
- inconsistent incident follow-up
- limited use of reliability data in decisions

## Evidence To Collect

- SLO dashboards
- incident summaries
- post-incident review actions
- service ownership map
