# Resilience Governance

## Purpose

Resilience governance defines who makes decisions, how exceptions are managed, and how improvements are tracked across the resilience program.

## Governance Questions

- Who owns the resilience program?
- Which leaders approve policy or exception changes?
- How are maturity findings reviewed?
- How do teams track closure of high-priority actions?

## Governance Practices

- maintain a visible ownership model
- review maturity results on a recurring cadence
- keep policy and exception logs current
- tie improvement actions to business risk

## Evidence To Collect

- governance charter
- policy review records
- steering committee notes
- exception register
- action tracker
