# Level 1: Initial

## Description

Practices are ad hoc, inconsistent, or mostly undocumented.

## Characteristics

- ownership is unclear
- evidence is limited
- reporting is reactive
- improvement actions are not tracked consistently

## Typical Risks

- repeated incidents
- unclear recovery expectations
- uncontrolled spend growth
- unverified controls

## Use

Use this level when practices are mostly ad hoc and evidence is limited.
