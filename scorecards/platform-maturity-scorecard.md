# Platform Maturity Scorecard

## Purpose

Assess the platform layer that supports resilience, reliability, and governance.

## Areas

- platform ownership
- standardization
- deployment consistency
- observability support
- resilience controls

## Interpretation

- low maturity means platforms are inconsistent and hard to support
- mid maturity means standard patterns exist but are uneven
- high maturity means the platform supports repeatable operations

## Use

Use this scorecard to show how well the platform layer supports resilience and governance outcomes.
