# Enterprise Resilience Scorecard

## Purpose

This scorecard gives leaders a simple view of resilience maturity across the operating stack.

## Use

Use this scorecard to combine governance, reliability, DR, observability, and FinOps into one executive-ready resilience view.

## Scorecard Areas

### 1. Governance

- policy clarity
- ownership model
- decision cadence

### 2. Reliability

- SLO discipline
- incident response maturity
- service ownership

### 3. Disaster Recovery

- RTO/RPO alignment
- recovery test success
- failover readiness

### 4. Observability

- signal coverage
- anomaly detection
- response automation

### 5. FinOps

- cost visibility
- budget variance control
- optimization action closure

## Example Summary

| Area | Target | Current | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Governance | Defined operating model | Partially defined | Needs Action |
| Reliability | Measured and improving | Measured | Healthy |
| Disaster Recovery | Tested and validated | In progress | At Risk |
| Observability | Predictive and actionable | Defined | Needs Action |
| FinOps | Controlled spend and forecasts | Managed | Healthy |

## Operating Rhythm

Recommended cadence:

1. Monthly resilience review
2. Quarterly maturity reassessment
3. Semiannual roadmap update
4. Annual target reset

## Recommended Actions

- focus on the lowest-scoring domain first
- use the maturity levels to show progression
- align roadmap priorities to risk and impact
- keep the scorecard lightweight enough for executive review

## Scorecard Outcome

The scorecard should make the resilience story simple enough for leadership and detailed enough for follow-through.
